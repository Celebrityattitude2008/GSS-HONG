# Student Portal User Guide — GSS Hong

This guide explains how students use the GSS Hong online portal to view and print their term results.

---

## Accessing the Portal

1. Visit the GSS Hong website
2. Click **"Student Portal"** in the navigation bar

---

## Signing In

You need two things to sign in:

1. **Admission Number** — the number issued to you by the school office, in the format `GSS/YYYY/NNNNN` (e.g. `GSS/2024/00231`)
2. **Password** — your default password when your account is first created is **`Password1`** (capital P, digit 1)

Enter both and click **"Sign In to Portal"**.

---

## First Login — Change Your Password

After signing in for the first time:

1. Click the **"Change Password"** button at the top of the page
2. Enter a new password (minimum 6 characters)
3. Confirm the new password
4. Click **"Update Password"**

Keep your new password safe. If you forget it, contact the school office to have it reset.

---

## Viewing Your Results

Once signed in, your released term results will be displayed. You can:

- Use the **term selector** at the top to switch between terms (e.g. First Term 2024/2025)
- See your **subject scores**, totals, and WAEC grades in the result table
- See your **overall position** and **class average** (when published by the admin)

If no results are shown yet, it means the school office has not released them yet. Check back later.

---

## Printing a Result Slip

1. Select the term you want to print
2. Click the **"Print Result"** button
3. Your browser's print dialog will open
4. Select your printer or choose "Save as PDF"
5. The result is formatted for **A4 paper** (portrait)

> **Tip:** For a clean print, turn off "Headers and footers" in your browser's print settings.

---

## Signing Out

Click **"Log Out"** at the top of the page when you are done. This is especially important if you are using a shared or public computer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My results are not showing. What should I do?**

Results are only visible after the school office has officially released them. If you believe your results should be available, contact the Results & Records office at `results@gsshong.edu.ng`.

**I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**

Contact the school office. A staff member can reset your password to the default `Password1` and you can change it again when you log in.

**My admission number doesn't work. What should I do?**

Make sure you are entering it in the correct format: `GSS/YYYY/NNNNN` (e.g. `GSS/2024/00231`). If it still doesn't work, your account may not have been created yet — contact the school office.

**Can I print results on my phone?**

Yes. The portal works on mobile browsers. When you tap "Print Result", your phone will offer options such as "Save as PDF" or send to a nearby printer.
